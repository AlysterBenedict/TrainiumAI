The Intelligence

Three AI Models.
One Unified System.

Trainium runs three specialized neural networks on-device, orchestrated together to deliver a seamless coaching experience.

01

Biometrics Estimator

A dual-branch EfficientNet regression model processes frontal and side-view photos through background removal and silhouette extraction to predict 15+ anthropometric measurements. Trained on the BodyM dataset with CV2 preprocessing.

ArchitectureEfficientNet Dual-Branch
Preprocessingrembg + CV2 Silhouette
Output15+ Body Measurements
02

Workout Transformer

A causal decoder-only Transformer takes tokenized biometric profiles and autoregressively generates 30-day workout sequences. Trained with mixed-precision (AMP) and gradient accumulation on 100K synthetic profiles with Top-K (k=10) sampling at inference.

ArchitectureCausal Decoder-Only Transformer
Training Data100,000 Synthetic Profiles
SamplingTop-K (k=10) Autoregressive
03

On-Device LLM Coach

Gemma 4 runs entirely on your phone via LiteRT, providing an intelligent conversational fitness coach. It has full context of your metrics, workout history, streaks, and calorie data — enabling personalized diet plans, motivation, and form tips in multiple languages.

ModelGemma 4 (LiteRT)
ContextFull User Profile + History
LanguagesMultilingual (EN, HI, KN, TA...)
